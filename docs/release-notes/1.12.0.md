In this release, agbsf2 converts the music of *The Santa Clause 3: The Escape Clause*. The game plays it with Engine Software's driver. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D1's, Nintendo R&D2's, AlphaDream's, Brownie Brown's, Ubisoft Milan's, Logik State's, Webfoot Technologies' and Engine Software's drivers, Frontline Studios' MOD and XM-like players, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.11.0

### Engine Software's driver

- agbsf2 now finds Engine Software's driver and converts its songs to MIDI and SoundFont. The driver plays modules in the manner of FastTracker 2 through a software mixer.
- *The Santa Clause 3: The Escape Clause* is supported, with its 12 songs. Each song starts as the game starts it: the music volume rises from 0 over the first 53 frames, and song 6 starts at full volume. Song 5 is a jingle that plays once and ends. Song 6 plays once too: the game fades it out before it loops.
- The volume envelopes and the fade-outs of the instruments are in the expression (CC11) of each note in the MIDI file. The SoundFont's zones contain no envelope and no fade-out. The SoundFont played alone therefore plays each instrument at a steady level.
- *Dragon Ball Z: Taiketsu* and *Ty the Tasmanian Tiger 2: Bush Rescue* have other builds of the driver. agbsf2 finds the driver in them and gives an error.
- `--driver engine_software` uses only this driver. `--dump` gives an error for its songs: there's no listing of them yet.
- The game's sound effects are single samples that its code starts. They aren't converted.
- The README's "Accuracy" section lists where a conversion differs from the game, under "Engine Software's driver".

### Help text

- The program's help now names Webfoot Technologies' driver and Frontline Studios' two players in its list of drivers. It left them out before.

## Known limitations

- *The Santa Clause 3*: the conversion starts the ramp of the music volume a frame later than the game does. In a song's first 53 frames, the music volume is one step of 4/256 below the game's. Songs 5 and 6 aren't affected.
- The limitations listed for 1.9.0 still apply. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
