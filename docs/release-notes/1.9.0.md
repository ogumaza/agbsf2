In this release, agbsf2 reads two more sound drivers: Nintendo R&D1's, in *WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!* and *Rhythm Tengoku*, and AlphaDream's, in *Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga* and two *Tottoko Hamtaro* games. It also reads the version of MP2K in *Metroid Fusion* and *Metroid: Zero Mission*, more revisions of Konami's and Quintet's drivers and more games with Krawall. The music of many games now plays at the volumes and with the driver settings that their code sets. With MP2K and Konami's, Nintendo R&D1's and Nintendo R&D2's drivers, a song without a loop ends at its last sound. Conversions with Ubisoft Milan's driver follow the PSG frame by frame, and *The Mummy* converts. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D1's, Nintendo R&D2's, AlphaDream's, Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.8.2

### Nintendo R&D1's driver

*WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!* and *Rhythm Tengoku* play their music and sound effects with a driver of Nintendo R&D1's. Each song is a Standard MIDI File, and the driver plays it with the instruments of a bank in the game. *Rhythm Tengoku* has a later revision of the driver. agbsf2 now reads both revisions.

- Each entry of the song table converts as a song. The sound effects convert with the music. `--driver rd1` selects the driver.
- The MIDI file keeps the notes on the ticks of the song file. The tempo map puts each tick at the time when the driver plays it. The driver steps through a song at a whole number of beats a minute, a frame at a time. The markers `[` and `]` become `loopStart` and `loopEnd`. A song without them plays once.
- Each channel that plays notes gets a track. The level and pan of the channel become CC11 and CC10, and the bend and the pitch LFO become pitch bends. Each note-on restarts the LFO delay. The driver does the same.
- The mixer feeds its whole output back, through two filters, as an echo. Every sample note therefore sends the first setting of the echo to CC91. PSG notes play outside the mixer and send 0.
- The PSG volume that the driver sets reaches its full 15 at about two thirds of a voice level, and it moves in whole steps. The velocity of each PSG note follows that volume. The driver plays a PSG note on the left side, on both sides or on the right side, by the CC10 of the channel. The MIDI file writes CC10 0, 64 or 127 for such a note.
- Square 1 can sweep its pitch. A swept note bends by the steps of the sweep. While the note sounds, the driver writes nothing more to the channel, and the note keeps the volume of its start. A rising sweep can turn the channel off. The note then ends there.
- A sample record of type `0x46` plays at the mixer rate of 13379 Hz on every key, whatever the rate in the header. The noise plays the entry of the driver table for each key from 21 to 80. Its samples are at 32768 Hz, the rate at which the GBA samples its PSG in these games.
- In the frame of a note's end, the driver still writes the PSG volume from before the release. A PSG note whose release the driver plays now ends a frame later, and its velocity includes the release.
- Each SoundFont has a preset for each program that the song plays, in the bank of the song record. The volume envelope follows the straight steps of level that the driver makes each frame. The driver's release takes longer from a higher level. A sample zone's release therefore lasts as long as the driver's from the mean level at which the song's notes on the zone end. The generators that all the zones of an instrument share move to the global zone of the instrument. With `--single-sf2`, the SoundFont of *WarioWare, Inc.* then stays within the limits of the format.

### AlphaDream's driver

*Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga* plays the music of the main game with a driver of AlphaDream's. *Tottoko Hamtaro 4: Nijiiro Daikoushin Dechu* and *Tottoko Hamtaro: Hamu-Hamu Sports* play their music with two other versions of the driver. agbsf2 now reads all three versions.

- Each entry of the song table converts as a song. The game starts its music on player 1, and the conversion plays each song there. `--driver alphadream` selects the driver. Detection reads the version from the routine that runs a track.
- Tracks 0 to 7 play samples through the software mixer, and tracks 8 to 11 play the PSG. The MIDI file has a track for each one that plays notes: `Track 0` to `Track 7`, `Square 1`, `Square 2`, `Wave` and `Noise`.
- The levels that the driver sets for the two sides of a channel become CC10 and CC11. The bend and fine tune become pitch bends. A tie bends the sounding note to the new key.
- The squares and the noise follow the envelope of the hardware. Their notes last while the channel sounds, and CC11 follows the mean volume of each frame. Their zones release at once.
- The squares and the wave bend to the frequency setting that the driver pitch table sets for each key. Keys below 36 get the setting 0, and a square plays them at 64 Hz. The wave plays an octave below a square at the same setting. It plays at 25, 50, 75 or 100%, by the channel volume at the start of the note.
- A song loops when its tracks jump back. The loop starts on the frame on which the last track first jumps back. The loop lasts as long as the most tracks fill with whole passes of their loops.
- `--dump` lists the commands of each song in the format of its version.

### MP2K

- *Metroid Fusion* and *Metroid: Zero Mission* play their music with a rework of the sequencer in the SDK library, with other envelopes and other PSG code. agbsf2 now finds and models that sequencer. *Metroid: Zero Mission* has no `m4aSongNumStart`, and its songs now convert.
- The two Metroid games count the envelope of a PSG channel a level at a time, in software. They restart a square or the noise channel only when a phase starts or the volume changes, and the hardware envelope moves the level in between. The conversion now follows that envelope. The velocities of the PSG notes in these games follow it.
- agbsf2 now reads the mixer settings of *Metroid Fusion*: 10512 Hz and 6 DirectSound channels, in stereo. The conversion assumed 13379 Hz and 8 channels before.
- Detection read the table of music players only up to the first entry of zeros. *Advance Wars* starts its table with such an entry, and every song played all its tracks. *Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories* has such entries later in its table, and the conversion left out most of its sound effects. An entry of zeros is now a player with no tracks.
- *Fire Emblem* and *Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones* start some songs with 8 DirectSound channels and the others with 7. agbsf2 converted every song with 7 and left out notes that the game plays. Detection now checks the routine that sets the number, and each song converts with the number that the game sets. `--info` lists the songs and the routine.
- A game can set the driver mode again after the init. Detection now reads a mode that the game sets with a literal straight after the init. *Guilty Gear X: Advance Edition*, *Tactics Ogre: The Knight of Lodis* and *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance* set the master volume that way. *F-Zero: GP Legend* sets the number of DirectSound channels, and *Mega Man Battle Network 5: Team Protoman* sets the reverb.
- agbsf2 also contains the modes that these games set in other ways: *Spyro: Season of Flame*, *Sugar Sugar Rune: Heart ga Ippai! Moegi Gakuen*, *Futari wa Precure Max Heart: Maji Maji! Fight de IN Janai*, *Futari wa Precure: Arienaai Yume no Kuni wa Daimeikyuu*, *The Cheetah Girls*, *The Proud Family* and *Strawberry Shortcake: Sweet Dreams*.
- *Kuru Kuru Kururin* sets the number of DirectSound channels for each song from a table. Its songs now convert with those numbers.
- Some games set the DirectSound channels, the reverb or the volume of a player when they start a song. agbsf2 contains these settings for *The Barbie Diaries: High School Mystery*, *The Little Mermaid: Magic in Two Kingdoms*, *Mr. Driller 2*, *Strawberry Shortcake: Ice Cream Island Riding Camp* and *Steel Empire*.
- *Mario Tennis: Power Tour* has a later version of Camelot's driver. The mixer mixes 12 DirectSound channels, whatever number the game sets. A player of priority 120 sets the priority of its notes to 255. A fixed-pitch voice can play a sample of 4-bit steps. The SoundFont contains the points that the mixer decodes from the steps. The echo is weaker than in *Golden Sun: The Lost Age*. Detection reads its level from the mixer code, and the tracks get a reverb send of 32.
- The driver sets a PSG note to a whole level from 0 to 15. The wave channel plays that level at one of five volumes: silence, 25%, 50%, 75% or 100%. The MIDI file used the level before the rounding. The velocity of each PSG note is now scaled by the level at which the PSG plays the note, over the frames of the note until its sound ends. The SoundFonts don't change.
- The README now lists *Pokémon Ruby Version*, *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team*, *Fire Emblem: The Binding Blade*, *Fire Emblem*, *Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones*, *Metroid Fusion* and *Metroid: Zero Mission* as supported.

### Konami's driver

agbsf2 now reads four more revisions of the driver. None of these games converted before.

| Revision | Games |
|---|---|
| Captain Tsubasa | *Tennis no Ouji-sama*, *Kami no Kijutsu: Illusion of the Evil Eyes*, *Captain Tsubasa: Eikou no Kiseki* and *Groove Adventure Rave* |
| Hunter X Hunter | *Hunter X Hunter: Minna Tomodachi Daisakusen!!*, *Tennis no Ouji-sama 2003: Passion Red* and *Cool Blue*, and *Tennis no Ouji-sama: Aim at the Victory!* |
| Kitaro | *Gegege no Kitarou: Kikiippatsu! Youkai Rettou* |
| Minna no Ouji-sama | *Minna no Ouji-sama* |

- The duty commands of the Kitaro revision store a duty of 0 to 3 in the low bits of the byte. The duty bits stay 0, and every square note plays at a duty of 12.5%.
- In the Minna no Ouji-sama revision, `FB` sets a song volume. At the end of each frame of a running track, the sequencer scales the track volume by the song volume. Vibrato stops while the song volume isn't full.
- *Tennis no Ouji-sama 2004: Glorious Gold* and *Stylish Silver* and *Mermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch: Pichi Pichitto Live Start!* have later builds of the *Ultimate Masters Edition* revision. agbsf2 converted them as that revision, and read their duty commands, delays, calls and other commands wrongly. agbsf2 now reads the two builds as separate revisions.
- These builds can pack a sample into single points, pairs and runs. agbsf2 now unpacks such a sample in the way the mixer plays it. Some songs of *Pichi Pichitto Live Start!* play one. Their notes were left out before.
- The live stages of *Mermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch* and *Pichi Pichitto Live Start!* change how some songs sound. They silence a track from a point in the song, or swap some notes for other samples. Each such song now gets a second pair of files, such as `game_02_live.mid` and `game_02_live.sf2`, with the version that the live stage plays.
- In *Hikaru no Go 2*, the routine that loads a wave doesn't check for volume 0 first. Detection found no wave table there, and the wave channel played the default waves. Detection now reads the routine in both forms.
- *Yu-Gi-Oh! Double Pack 2* contains a build of the Dungeon Dice Monsters revision whose code uses other registers. Detection now finds the driver tables in that build.
- The README now lists *Animal Yokochou: Doki Doki Kyuushutsu Daisakusen no Maki*, *Mermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch*, *Groove Adventure Rave: Hikari to Yami no Daikessen 2*, *Hikaru no Go*, *Hikaru no Go 2*, *Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters 6 Expert 2*, *GetBackers Dakkanya: Jigoku no Scaramouche* and *Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters 5 Expert 1* as supported.

### Quintet's driver

- Many games with the later revision keep their songs, sound effects and tables in music files. A routine of the driver loads a file into the driver tables. Detection now finds that routine and reads each file that the game passes to it. A scan of the ROM finds the other files, among them the files of the programs that a game sends to another Game Boy Advance. The songs and sound effects of each file are numbered after those of the file before.
- These games now convert: *Blender Bros.*, *Robopon 2: Ring Version* and *Cross Version*, *Bouken Yuuki Plaston GP*, *Bouken Yuuki Densetsu Plaston Gate*, *Bouken Yuuki PlaStar World: Densetsu no Plust Gate EX*, *Legend of Dynamic: Goushouden*, *Doubutsu no Shima no Chobi Gurumi*, *Gachasute! Dyna Device Red* and *Blue*, and *Magical Fengshen*.
- *Suta Komi: Star Communicator* has the S revision of the driver, with another song format. A note has two bytes, the pitch and then the note value. Detection failed on the game before. agbsf2 now reads the S revision.

### Krawall

The README now lists these games as supported:

| Build | Games |
|---|---|
| the build dated 2003/09/01 | *The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age*, *The Urbz: Sims in the City*, *Robots* and *Franklin the Turtle* |
| the build dated 2004/11/14 | *Care Bears: Care Quest*, *The Koala Brothers: Outback Adventures* and *The Land Before Time: Into the Mysterious Beyond* |
| the build dated 2005-04-21 | *Cocoto Kart Racer*, *Cocoto Platform Jumper*, *Let's Ride! Friends Forever*, *Let's Ride! Dreamer*, *Crash of the Titans*, *Scurge: Hive* and *Shrek SuperSlam* |

- *Cocoto Kart Racer* and *Franklin's Great Adventures* contain a second copy of the first values of the vibrato ramp, before the player tables. Detection used the first copy and failed. The ramp is now the copy that the square wave follows.
- *Cocoto Platform Jumper* and the two *Let's Ride!* games mix 552 samples a call, where other games mix 276. Detection now reads that number and finds the mixer rate. Both *Cocoto* games and both *Let's Ride!* games now convert. So does *Horse and Pony: Let's Ride 2*, the European release of *Let's Ride! Dreamer*.
- Detection now reads direct calls of `krapPlay()` in the game code. A module that no such call loops plays once, and its MIDI file stops at the end of the module. *Cocoto Kart Racer* plays some modules that way. `--info` lists them.
- The start-up code of *Franklin the Turtle* loads the address of `kragInit()` and then sets the argument. Detection now reads that form. The game sets a mono mixer, and every channel of the conversion now plays in the centre.
- *Franklin's Great Adventures* plays each entry of its table of modules in a mode and at a music volume from two more tables. Detection now reads the routine that plays the entries. Each entry converts in the mode and at the volume that the tables set. The entries in mode 0 play once.
- Detection read the master volume from the first call of `kramSetMasterVol()` in the ROM. In *Shrek SuperSlam*, that call is in the pause code, and the whole game converted at a quarter of the master volume that the game sets. Detection now uses the call that follows the sound init.
- The conversion now plays the music of many more games at the music volume that the code of the game sets. Among them are *Digimon Racing*, *Crash of the Titans*, *The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age*, *Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban*, *Marvel: Ultimate Alliance*, *The Legend of Spyro: The Eternal Night* and the *Uno* games, with their releases in other regions. These games played at the library default of 128 before.
- Some games set the volume or the mode for single pieces. *Care Bears: Care Quest* and *The Koala Brothers: Outback Adventures* play the music of their levels at 64. *Franklin the Turtle* plays the pieces of its mini-games at 64. *Cocoto Kart Racer* and *Cocoto Platform Jumper* set a volume for each piece. *Postman Pat and the Greendale Rocket* sets a level for each entry of its table of modules. *The Bee Game* plays its modules in song mode.
- In a module with more than 15 channels that play notes, the channels whose settings clash least now share a MIDI channel. Two notes clash if they sound at once with another pan, expression or bend, or on the same key. Where a note of one channel on a shared MIDI channel ends, the other channel sets its pan, expression and bend again. In such a module, a note now ends where the mixer keeps its levels at 0 for the rest of the note. The expression of another channel could make such a note sound again before. The warning states the time at which two channels clash. A module with no more than 15 channels that play notes converts as before.

### Ubisoft Milan's driver

- *The Mummy* uses the engine of *Tomb Raider: The Prophecy* and plays its music mostly on the PSG. The conversion now follows the PSG frame by frame: the hardware envelopes, the length counters, the square 1 sweep, the noise, legato notes, and the modulation and glide of each instrument. Before, a PSG note kept its starting volume, and the conversion left out the envelope, the modulation and the glides.
- A PSG note plays at full velocity. CC11 sets its level each frame, and the pitch bend follows the modulation and the glides. The squares and the wave play zones tuned to the frequency table of the driver.
- The noise notes of channel 9 move to a `Noise` track on MIDI channel 4. Each note plays a drum in bank 3 that contains the sound that the noise channel makes from the note-on: its envelope, its length and any changes that the driver writes. Before, a noise note ended at its note-off.
- *The Mummy* switches the kit of channel 9 off before most levels start their music, and can switch it on later. Songs 1 to 15 now convert without the kit. A second pair of files for each of these songs, such as `game_01_kit.mid` and `game_01_kit.sf2`, contains the version with the kit. The game plays the other songs with the kit.
- *Tomb Raider: The Prophecy* plays nine long samples as music, with no sequence. They now convert as songs 14 to 22. Each pass of a sample loop is a note on the `Kit` track, on a key from 81 up.
- *The Sum of All Fears* and *Rogue Spear* have an older build of the driver, and their music is recorded samples. agbsf2 now reports "Found an older build of Ubisoft Milan's sound driver. The music is recorded samples, with no sequences to convert." Before, it reported that it found no sound driver.

### Rare's driver

- The code of each game sets a music volume for each tune. The mixer scales every note of the tune by that volume. agbsf2 now contains the starting volume of each tune in the supported games, with the options as they are when the first tune starts. Each MIDI channel starts with that volume as expression: 127 × √(volume/128). The conversions played every tune at 128 before.
- A build that agbsf2 doesn't list plays at 128, or at 69 in the revision of *Donkey Kong Country 3*, with a warning.
- In *Donkey Kong Country 3*, tune 2 starts at a lower music volume. The mixer then also skips the notes of a channel at volume 2 or less. The conversion now does the same.

### Nintendo R&D2's driver

- *Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3* has the revision of *Super Mario Advance 3*. The README now lists it as supported.

### Songs without a loop

- With MP2K and Konami's, Nintendo R&D1's and Nintendo R&D2's drivers, a song without a loop ended where its last track ended. A track can wait, or change a controller, after its last note. In Konami's driver, a track can rest for a long time before it stops the song. Some files ran on long after the last sound. When the end of the song is more than 10 s after its last sound has died away, the MIDI file now ends where that sound dies away.

### Other changes

- The ROM and GSF reader, MP2K, Konami's driver and their conversions now come from ntdlib. The repository includes ntdlib as a third Git submodule, in `externals/ntdlib`. In a clone from before this release, run `git submodule update --init` before you build.
- `--driver` accepts `rd1` and `alphadream`. Detection looks for Nintendo R&D1's and AlphaDream's drivers after Nintendo R&D2's, before MP2K.
- For a game with no known driver, the message names all ten drivers that detection looks for.

## Known limitations

- With Nintendo R&D1's driver, the conversion plays each song as its MIDI file is written. *WarioWare, Inc.* speeds the music of a stage up as the stage goes on. The scripts of *Rhythm Tengoku* change the speed, the pitch and the volume, and silence groups of tracks in some games.
- With Nintendo R&D1's driver, the reverb of a SoundFont player stands in for the echo of the mixer. Notes play at their written pitch where CC82 and CC83 set a random pitch or key in the game. A voice whose right side CC75 inverts plays as usual.
- With AlphaDream's driver, the conversion plays each song as the game starts it, at full volume and the driver tempo. The game fades its music in and out, plays it at half volume in some scenes and changes the tempo of song 29 in one cutscene. Sound effects take a track from songs 7 and 40 while they play.
- With AlphaDream's driver, a track whose loop length differs from the song loop jumps at each repeat in a player that loops on the markers. Between semitones, the mixer moves in a straight line in frequency, where the bends follow a curve. The two differ by up to 2 cents.
- With MP2K, a PSG note that would need a velocity above 127 plays at 127, quieter than in the game. Below 100%, the wave channel drops the fraction of each point of the pattern. The SoundFont plays the pattern unrounded, and the wave of the game sounds coarser.
- With Konami's driver, *Hunter X Hunter: Minna Tomodachi Daisakusen!!* requests some songs with a list or a table of samples that it builds as it runs. The conversion plays those songs with the samples that their notes name.
- Like *The Sims: Bustin' Out*, *The Urbz: Sims in the City*, *Robots* and *Shrek SuperSlam* play their music at 255. Their conversions play at 128, 6 dB quieter than the game, with the same balance.
- With Krawall, agbsf2 contains the music volumes of the releases that its tables list, by game code. Another release, such as one from another region, plays at 128. A piece that the game plays at two volumes converts at one of them.
- With Ubisoft Milan's driver, the game keeps the modulation, glide and wave of a PSG channel from one piece to the next. The conversion starts each piece from the state after the driver init.
- With `--single-sf2`, the instruments of some games don't fit in one SoundFont, among them *Pokémon Ruby Version*, *Pokémon FireRed Version*, *Pokémon Emerald*, *Mario Kart: Super Circuit*, *Groove Adventure Rave*, *Hikaru no Go*, *Yu-Gi-Oh! The Eternal Duelist Soul*, *Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters 6 Expert 2* and *Rhythm Tengoku*. agbsf2 stops with "soundfont too large". Convert these games without the option.
- In a GSF rip, a sample that the game plays at a high pitch can have gaps. At a high pitch the game skips some bytes of the sample, and the rip keeps only the bytes that the game read. agbsf2 doesn't fill the gaps with a guess. Use a ROM if you have one.
- Apart from the PSG of Ubisoft Milan's driver and the samples that *Tomb Raider: The Prophecy* plays as music, the limitations listed for 1.8.2 still apply. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
