In this release, the squares and the wave of Nintendo R&D2's driver play at the driver's frequency table, and three games with Konami's driver lose reverb that they don't play. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D1's, Nintendo R&D2's, AlphaDream's, Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.9.3

### Konami's driver

- In *Pichi Pichitto Live Start!* and *Tennis no Ouji-sama 2004: Glorious Gold* and *Stylish Silver*, the game's mixer takes only voices 5 to 11 from echo bus 0. The MIDI files also sent reverb (CC91) to voices 0 to 4. Those voices now play without it. This changes 67 of the 72 MIDI files of *Live Start!* and 4 of the 28 of *Glorious Gold*.

### Nintendo R&D2's driver

- The squares and the wave now play at the settings of the driver's frequency table. They played in equal temperament before. The SoundFonts' square and wave zones get fine tunes. Most notes move by less than 3 cents. About one in eight moves by 3 to 6 cents, and about one in fourteen by more. This affects *Super Mario Advance 2*, *3* and *4*. The music of *A Link to the Past* has no square or wave instruments and doesn't change.

### Windows

- agbsf2 now runs with UTF-8 as its code page. An image or a folder whose name the system's code page can't hold now opens.

### Libraries

- agbsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.4.0, xsflib 1.10.0 and ntdlib 3.6.1.

## Known limitations

The limitations listed for 1.9.0 still apply. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
