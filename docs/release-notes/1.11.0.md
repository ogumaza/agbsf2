In this release, agbsf2 converts the music of the Dragon Ball Z games with Webfoot Technologies' driver and of five games with Frontline Studios' two players. The short loops of *Zapper* and *Pinball Challenge Deluxe* now play at the pitch of the game, some songs with Quintet's driver loop where the game does, and notes that Nintendo R&D2's driver cuts off stop where the game stops them. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D1's, Nintendo R&D2's, AlphaDream's, Brownie Brown's, Ubisoft Milan's, Logik State's and Webfoot Technologies' drivers, Frontline Studios' MOD and XM-like players, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.10.0

### Webfoot Technologies' driver

- agbsf2 now finds Webfoot Technologies' driver and converts its songs to MIDI and SoundFont. The driver plays Impulse Tracker modules in a packed form. It has two builds.
- *Dragon Ball Z: The Legacy of Goku*, *Dragon Ball Z: The Legacy of Goku II* and *Dragon Ball Z: Buu's Fury* are supported, and so is the cartridge with *Buu's Fury* and *Dragon Ball GT: Transformation*. That cartridge contains three programs. Song 0 is the menu music, songs 1-53 come from *Buu's Fury* and songs 54-78 from *GT Transformation*. `--info` gives each program's song table and range of songs.
- Each song plays at the music volume that the game starts with: 4 of 8, and 3 of 8 in *GT Transformation*. `--music-volume N` converts at another volume, from 1 to 8. Without `--driver`, the option now looks for Logik State's and Webfoot Technologies' drivers, and no longer implies `--driver logikstate`.
- `--driver webfoot` uses only this driver. `--dump` gives an error for its songs: there's no listing of them yet.
- The games' sound effects are single samples that their code starts. They aren't converted.
- The README's "Accuracy" section lists where a conversion differs from the game, under "Webfoot Technologies' driver".

### Frontline Studios' MOD player

- agbsf2 now finds Frontline Studios' MOD player and converts its songs to MIDI and SoundFont. The player plays one module of four channels for all the music of a game, and the game starts each song at an order of the module.
- *Hardcore Pool*, *Garfield: The Search for Pooky* and *Games Explosion!* are supported. *Garfield* has two modules: songs 0-10 come from the first and songs 11-17 from the second. `--info` gives the range of songs of each module and the order of each song.
- A part of a module that the game never starts becomes a song too, after the game's songs of the same module. `--info` marks those songs.
- *Games Explosion!* fades most of its songs in over eight rows, and the conversion plays the fade-in.
- `--driver frontline_mod` uses only this player. `--dump` gives an error for its songs: there's no listing of them yet.
- The games' sound effects are single samples that their code starts. They aren't converted.
- The README's "Accuracy" section lists where a conversion differs from the game, under "Frontline Studios' MOD player".

### Frontline Studios' XM-like player

- agbsf2 now finds Frontline Studios' XM-like player and converts its songs to MIDI and SoundFont. The player plays modules of four channels, and the game plays each module through an order list that the game supplies.
- *Chicken Shoot* and *Chicken Shoot 2* are supported, with five songs each: the title music and the lists for the levels of the game. `--info` gives the module, the list and the master volume of each song.
- Each song plays at the master volume that the game passes on a new save.
- `--driver frontline_xm` uses only this player. `--dump` gives an error for its songs: there's no listing of them yet.
- The games' sound effects aren't converted.
- The README's "Accuracy" section lists where a conversion differs from the game, under "Frontline Studios' XM-like player".

### Logik State's driver

- In *Zapper: One Wicked Cricket!* and *Pinball Challenge Deluxe*, the mixer drops the fraction of its place in a sample each time a loop starts again. A short loop therefore plays lower than the pitch of its sample, by up to 158 cents. The MIDI files now bend those notes to the pitch the game plays. This changes 151 of the 307 MIDI files of *Pinball Challenge Deluxe* and 18 of the 19 of *Zapper*. The SoundFonts don't change.
- The European release of *Zapper* now plays at the music volume that it sets, 32 of 64.
- agbsf2 now warns when it can't read the music volume of a game from the game's code, or the slides to a note of a game with `GBAMOD1.` modules. The music then plays at the defaults of the driver.
- `--music-volume` now takes a volume from 1 to 256 for the games with `GBAMOD30` modules. Five of them play their music above 64. The games with `GBAMOD2.` and `GBAMOD1.` modules still take 1 to 64.

### Quintet's driver

- 27 songs of nine games now loop where the game repeats them. The loops of 8 songs start one pass earlier, and the loops of 19 songs start one pass later. Only the loop markers and the lengths of the MIDI files change.

### Nintendo R&D2's driver

- When a new note takes a sample voice that still sounds, the game stops the old note at once. The SoundFont used to play the release of the old note on. In *Super Mario Advance 4*'s sequence 32, a release rang on under the music every 2.45 s. Where nothing else of its track sounds, the MIDI files now stop such a note with All Sound Off (CC120). This changes 165 MIDI files of the four games with the driver. The SoundFonts don't change.
- *Super Mario Advance 4* now starts its songs from the order of free voices that the game's start-up leaves. In 13 of its MIDI files, up to 23 CC11 values change. No note changes.

### MP2K

- A song whose notes all play at a level of 0, such as the notes of a track at a volume of 0, makes no sound. agbsf2 now skips it. It already skipped a song without notes. Song 50 of *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team* is one: it sets the volume to 0 and plays one quiet note. 55 songs of ten games are now skipped, among them six of *Mother 3*, 23 of *Metroid: Zero Mission* and eight of *WarioWare: Twisted!*.

### Rare's driver

- The prototype of *Battletoads* now plays its tunes at the music volumes that the game sets: 70 of 128 for tunes 0 and 1, and 80 for tune 2. Only the CC11 of each channel at the start of the MIDI files changes.

### Documentation

- The README and the docs of the drivers are reworded to read more plainly. The README's `--dump` section now covers Logik State's driver. The docs name agblib, not ntdlib, as the library with the drivers' code and tables.

### Libraries

- agbsf2 now builds on agblib 1.4.0, ntdlib 7.0.0, xsflib 1.12.0 and seqlib 1.5.0. The GBA code and the sound drivers now come from agblib. They came from ntdlib before.

## Known limitations

The limitations listed for 1.9.0 still apply. In Nintendo R&D2's driver, the release of a cut note still plays on where another note of its track sounds. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
