In this release, the music of *Tomb Raider: The Prophecy* plays at the game's pitch. It played an octave too high before. The square and wave notes of Konami's and Quintet's drivers and of MP2K follow the games' frequency tables. With Nintendo R&D2's driver, notes in legato keep the voice's envelope. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D2's, Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.8.0

### Ubisoft Milan's driver

- *Tomb Raider: The Prophecy*'s samples played an octave too high. Each loop lasted half as long. The game's sound init works out the mixer's rate with a routine of its floating-point library. agbsf2 took that routine for a multiplication. The routine adds 0.5. Timer 0's reload is then 0xFBFF. The mixer makes 16368 points a second. Each voice moves through its sample at 8184 Hz. The kit's zones now play each sample at that rate. `--info` gives the new rates and reload. `tools/ubimilan/driver_emu.py` uses the same reload.

### Square and wave notes

- The drivers play each key of a square or wave note at its entry in a frequency table. Quintet's table departs from equal temperament by up to 43 cents. In Konami's driver and MP2K, the 11-bit frequency registers round the high notes, by up to about 50 cents in the C7 octave. The SoundFont's square and wave zones played every key in equal temperament. They now follow the table key by key. Each run of keys that the table puts the same whole cents from equal temperament gets zones with that fine tune. The keys outside a table take the tuning of its nearest entry. The MIDI files stay the same.
- In the Dungeon Dice Monsters revision of Konami's driver, the bends follow each note's frequency setting. That revision's zones keep equal temperament. Brownie Brown's conversion already kept its game's tuning.

### MP2K

- The driver counts a PSG voice's attack, decay and release as whole bytes. The SoundFont took only their low 3 bits, and the low 4 bits of the sustain. A voice with a larger value got an envelope up to 42 times faster than the game's. The zones now follow the whole bytes. A sustain above 15 holds the full level.

### Nintendo R&D2's driver

- In legato, the driver plays a track's notes on one voice. The voice keeps its envelope from note to note. A SoundFont player started each note's envelope again. A track's sample notes in legato now play on a second preset of their instrument, named `Bank B legato N`. Its zones have only the region's release. The track's CC11 follows the voice's envelope frame by frame. Each note ends at the track's next note or at the voice's release.

## Known limitations

- With Konami's driver and MP2K, a bend moves a square or wave note's pitch from its table entry in equal temperament. The game plays the table's entry for the bent pitch.
- With Quintet's driver, PCM notes play in equal temperament. The driver's PCM pitch table lies within 7 cents of it, apart from its top entry. That entry is 14 cents flat.
- With Nintendo R&D2's driver, the PSG's notes keep equal-tempered keys.
- In legato, Nintendo R&D2's driver plays the new note's sample on from the point that the voice had reached in the last note. A SoundFont player starts each note's sample again. The MIDI file ends a note in legato where the next one starts. The note's release then sounds for a moment under the next note. The driver changes the voice's note at once.
- Apart from the equal-tempered square and wave notes of Konami's and Quintet's drivers and of MP2K, the limitations listed for 1.8.0 still apply. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
