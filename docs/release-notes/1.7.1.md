In this release, the conversions of *Super Mario Advance 2* leave out the tracks that the game mutes. Sequence 30 gets a pair of files for each of the two versions that the game plays. Sequences 15 to 18 get a pair without the track that plays while the player rides Yoshi and a pair with it. *Digimon Racing*'s jingle now converts once, without a loop. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D2's, Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.7.0

### Nintendo R&D2's driver

- *Super Mario Advance 2* plays sequence 30, a jingle, as music 17 and as music 18. Tracks 8 and 9 each hold a version of the jingle's last hit. The game mutes track 8 as music 17 and track 9 as music 18. A muted track plays no notes. The conversion used to play both tracks. The jingle then ended on both versions of its last hit at once. The sequence now gets two pairs of files: `game_30.mid` and `game_30.sf2` as music 17, and `game_30_music18.mid` and `game_30_music18.sf2` as music 18. Each pair leaves out the track that the game mutes.
- The game also mutes track 6 of sequences 15 to 18 unless the player rides Yoshi. The conversion used to play the track in every case. A new game starts without Yoshi. The first pair of each of those sequences, such as `game_15.mid` and `game_15.sf2`, now leaves the track out. A second pair, such as `game_15_yoshi.mid` and `game_15_yoshi.sf2`, plays it as the game does while the player rides Yoshi.
- `--info` lists the tracks that the game mutes in each sequence. Each version of a sequence gets a line of its own. The line gives the version's name after the loop: "as music 18" or "riding Yoshi". A conversion's messages give the version's name after the sequence's number.
- The README now says how *Super Mario Advance 2* uses the driver's echo. The game turns the echo on in some levels. No track of its music has an echo send. In those levels the echo reaches only the sound effects.

### Krawall

- *Digimon Racing* plays module 16 once, as a jingle. The conversion used to loop the module like the others. agbsf2 now finds the game's routine that plays a module once. The jingle's MIDI file plays the module to its end and stops. The file has no loop markers. Krawall plays a jingle that way while no other module plays. `--info` names the routine and lists the jingles.

## Known limitations

- The sound effects of Quintet's and Nintendo R&D2's drivers play on their own in their conversions. In the game, a sound effect of Quintet's driver takes each sound channel that it plays on from the music while it plays. A sound effect of Nintendo R&D2's driver and the music take voices from each other by their priorities.
- *Donkey Kong Country 3* fades Enchanted Riverbank from one arrangement to the other when the player dives in or climbs out. Each of the tune's files holds one arrangement from start to end. The game can also start a tune at a marker partway through. The conversion plays each tune from its start.
- With Nintendo R&D2's driver, the MIDI file changes a square's or the noise's CC11 at the start of a frame. The PSG's envelope steps the volume at 64 Hz. The hardware's step can then come up to a frame before the step in the MIDI file. At 25% and 50%, the game's wave plays a little quieter and coarser than the SoundFont's. Where a track's loudest velocity would take its CC11 above 127, the track's loudest notes play quieter than in the game.
- With Nintendo R&D2's driver, agbsf2 reads the tracks that a game mutes only from two routines of *Super Mario Advance 2*: the play-music routine and the routine for the track that plays while the player rides Yoshi. In another game, the MIDI file has every track.
- *Super Mario Advance 2* changes the tempo of its level music in some states of the game. The music speeds up once the timer falls under 100, for example. The conversion plays each sequence at the tempo that the game first starts it with.
- With Krawall, agbsf2 finds a jingle only through a routine in the form that *Digimon Racing* has. A jingle that a game plays through other code converts as a loop.
- Apart from the sound effects of Quintet's and Nintendo R&D2's drivers, the channels of *Donkey Kong Country 3*, the tracks that *Super Mario Advance 2* mutes and *Digimon Racing*'s jingle, the limitations listed for 1.6.0 still apply. See "Accuracy" and "Known issues" in the README for the details.
