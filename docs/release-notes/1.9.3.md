In this release, Nintendo R&D1's and AlphaDream's drivers come from ntdlib, and MP2K reads more of the ways a game sets the driver's mode. agbsf2 converts Game Boy Advance music to MIDI and SoundFont. It supports Konami's, Rare's, Quintet's, Nintendo R&D1's, Nintendo R&D2's, AlphaDream's, Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers, MP2K and Krawall.

The program runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It doesn't require any runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.gba`, `.gsflib` or `.minigsf` files on `agbsf2.exe`. You can also run it in a terminal. `agbsf2 --help` lists the options. Each file's output goes in a folder next to it.

On a Mac with Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. On macOS the build also makes `Agbsf2.app`. You can drop files on it in the Finder.

## Changes since 1.9.2

### MP2K

- A game can set the driver's mode right after `m4aSoundInit`, with a call of `m4aSoundMode`. agbsf2 read that mode only where the game loaded it with `ldr`, straight after the init. It now also reads a mode that the game builds with `movs` and `lsls`, and it steps over up to two calls of other routines before the first call of `m4aSoundMode`. Each call applies in turn. *Sugar Sugar Rune: Heart ga Ippai! Moegi Gakuen*, two *Futari wa Precure* games, *The Cheetah Girls* and *The Proud Family* set their modes that way. agbsf2 already had their settings in a table. Their conversions don't change.

### Nintendo R&D1's driver

- A tempo that gives the driver a step of 0 sends no tempo change. The driver keeps the step it had, and the MIDI file now does the same. Before, the file played on at its slowest tempo from there.
- The driver plays the notes of track *t* on channel *t* mod 16. A program change or a controller on track 16 or above sets a channel record that no note reads. The conversion gave it to the channel of track *t* − 16. It now leaves it out.

### AlphaDream's driver

- The `--dump` listings follow a goto forward to its target. They listed the bytes after the goto as the track's commands. They also call F7 a check for the end of a note. F7 ends no note, and the listings called it "release".

### Code from ntdlib

- Nintendo R&D1's and AlphaDream's drivers now come from ntdlib, as the other eight drivers already did. Apart from the changes above, their conversions are the same as in 1.9.2.
